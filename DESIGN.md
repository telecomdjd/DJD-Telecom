## Brand & Style

This design system translates the speed, low latency, and reliability of high-performance fiber optic broadband into an electric, immersive digital presence. Positioned at the intersection of high-tech infrastructure and seamless consumer accessibility, the interface evokes velocity, crystal-clear connectivity, and technical precision.

The design movement combines **Deep-Dark Modernism** with **Electroluminescent Glassmorphism**. The visual world is built on void-like deep graphite and navy tones (`#0A0D14`, `#0F172A`), illuminated by laser-thin conduits of pure cyan and electric cobalt light (`#0066FF`, `#00D2FF`, `#38BDF8`). The emotional goal is to eradicate feelings of buffering, downtime, and bureaucratic telecommunications, replacing them with a sensory impression of uninhibited bandwidth and immediate speed. Surfaces rely on layered translucent dark containers, subtle linear photon gradients, and controlled neon penumbras that signal fiber optics in active transmission.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively for a high-contrast dark environment. Light and kinetic energy are provided by saturated spectral blues and luminescent cyans, set over atmospheric dark fields.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#0070F3` / `#0284C7`)**: Electric Fiber Cobalt. Used for decisive transactional call-to-actions, core branded elements, and primary focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#00D2FF` / `#38BDF8`)**: Cyan Photon. Represents high speed, active ping states, highlight borders, and illuminated gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#22C55E` / `#25D366`)**: Signal Green & WhatsApp Native Accent. Reserved strictly for instant conversion hooks (WhatsApp contracting), affirmative operational statuses, and live support signals.
- **Neutral Base (`#0A0D14`)**: Void Graphite. The foundational backdrop, enriched with layered midnight navy undertones (`#0F172A`, `#131C2E`).

### Functional Contexts
- **Text & Foreground**:
  - `text-primary`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White, 100% contrast against dark surfaces)
  - `text-secondary`: `#94A3B8` (Silver Ash, used for body copy and technical specs)
  - `text-tertiary`: `#64748B` (Muted Slate, used for auxiliary metadata, terms, and input placeholders)
  - `text-accent`: `#38BDF8` (Active Cyan highlight)
- **Surfaces & Borders**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#0A0D14`
  - `surface-card`: `#111827` / `#131C2E` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
  - `surface-card-elevated`: `#1E293B`
  - `border-subtle`: `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`
  - `border-glow`: `rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.5)`

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to maintain geometric balance, technical legibility, and modern elegance.

### Metric and Numerical Rules
- **Monetary & Bandwidth Lockups**: Speed values (e.g., `600 MEGA`) and monetary figures (`R$ 69,90`) must leverage tight tabular tracking (`letterSpacing: -0.03em`) with `fontWeight: 800`.
- **Currency Anatomy**: The currency symbol (`R$`) and decimals (`,90`) are vertically aligned using `price-unit` styling, top-aligned or baseline-anchored relative to the prominent integer core (`price-display`), creating clear commercial hierarchy.
- **Micro-copy & Badges**: System status tags and plan tags use uppercase tracking (`label-sm`) with `letterSpacing: 0.05em` to mirror precision networking readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine utilizes a strict 8-point base module embedded within an adaptive 12-column layout container (maximum content width: 1280px).

### Breakpoints & Container Rules
- **Desktop (1024px - 1440px+)**: 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px), dynamic outer margin clamping at `margin: 2rem` to `4rem`. Plan comparison displays strictly across a 3-column configuration (`col-span-4` each).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, outer margins `1.5rem`. Plan matrix wraps or shifts to a horizontal swipe container.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns, `gutter-sm: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Plan tiers display as single-column stacks with prominent vertical separation (`space-lg`).

### Section Flow
Major landing sections (Hero, Planos, Cobertura, Como Contratar, FAQ) must maintain vertical breathing room using `space-3xl` (96px) on desktop and `space-2xl` (64px) on mobile devices to prevent optical density overload.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established without opaque skeuomorphic bevels. Instead, this system applies luminous ray propagation and dark-glass planar layering.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface 0 (Base Canvas)**: Solid `#0A0D14` with ambient radial gradients (`radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% -20%, rgba(0, 112, 243, 0.15), transparent 70%)`).
- **Surface 1 (Standard Card & Module)**: Tinted glass with background fill `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`, background blur `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bound by a 1px border `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.18)`.
- **Surface 2 (Interactive Hover / Featured Plan)**: Elevated container with fill `rgba(19, 28, 46, 0.9)`, border `rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.5)`, casting an atmospheric ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 25px -5px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.25), 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Surface 3 (Overlays, Flyouts, Fixed Navigation)**: High-density graphite glass `rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and bottom edge highlight `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Optical Neon Accents
Card boundaries use an internal optical sheen via an inset top border highlight: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced curvature model where outer boundaries retain structural discipline while interactive nodes stay approachable:

- **Large Structural Surfaces (`rounded-2xl` / 1.25rem - 1.5rem)**: Plan comparison cards, coverage search panel containers, and testimonial wrappers.
- **Controls & Modals (`rounded-lg` / 0.75rem - 1rem)**: Text input fields, accordion panels, dropdown menus, and nested metric chips.
- **Interactive Badges & Pill Buttons (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: Speed indicator tags, status pings (e.g., "Fibra Ativa"), and floating WhatsApp quick-action actions.

## Components

### 1. Plan Cards (Carrossel / Grid de Planos)
- **Container**: `rounded-2xl`, dark graphite canvas (`#131C2E`), 1px structural border (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`). The top portion integrates a vibrant gradient banner (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0070F3 0%, #00D2FF 100%)`) explicitly framing the speed capacity (e.g., "500 MEGA DUAL BAND").
- **Highlighted / Recommended Tier**: Enhanced with a persistent exterior ambient cyan aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 30px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.3)`), a glowing badge pinned top-center ("MAIS POPULAR"), and an active cyan border stroke (`#00D2FF`).
- **Pricing Block**: Giant numerals using `price-display` in `#FFFFFF`, with currency and periodicity configured via `price-unit` in `#94A3B8`.
- **Feature List**: Stacked vertical list with `space-sm` gaps, each item anchored by an electric-blue checkmark icon.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Contratar / Assinar)**: Deep gradient (`#0070F3` to `#0284C7`), text pure white, bold. Hover state triggers an expanded neon aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 112, 243, 0.6)`), transforming up 1px.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action**: Dedicated high-conversion variant using WhatsApp Emerald (`#25D366`), dark charcoal or white typography, accompanied by a clean SVG WhatsApp icon. Hover state projects an emerald energy halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px border `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`, text `#38BDF8`. Hover fills with `rgba(0, 112, 243, 0.1)`.

### 3. Inputs & Coverage Search (Consulta de Viabilidade)
- **Barra de Busca de Bairros**: An integrated command-bar aesthetic. Input field styled in `#0F172A` with an inner border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` that transitions instantly to `#00D2FF` on focus with an active cyan glow. Placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- **Result Tags**: Micro-chips marked with a live pulsing green dot (`#22C55E`) indicating "Fibra Disponível no seu Bairro".

### 4. Step-by-Step Flow (Como Contratar)
- **3 Connected Steps**: Interconnected with an optical horizontal gradient pipeline (vertical on mobile) symbolizing the flow of data photons.
- **Step Nodes**: Circular badge containing numeric index in high-contrast cyan, floating on a dark glass backing.

### 5. Accordion (FAQ & Central de Ajuda)
- **Items**: Enclosed graphite slabs (`#111827`) separated by `space-sm`. Subtle stroke `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`.
- **Expansion State**: Header text shifts from `#94A3B8` to `#38BDF8`, trailing chevron rotates 180 degrees smoothly, and expanded response text transitions in `#94A3B8` with an open left border in neon cyan.

### 6. Site Header & Footer
- **Header**: Floating fixed dock, blur level 20px, dark graphite translucency, showcasing brand mark with cyan fiber dot, rapid navigation links with cyan bottom-glow indicator on hover, and right-aligned "Área do Cliente" button.
- **Footer**: Deep tone `#070A0F`, structured columns (Planos, Institucional, Suporte, Jurídico), complete regulatory information, and a micro-status pill signaling "Rede 100% Operacional".
